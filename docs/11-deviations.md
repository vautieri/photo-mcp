# Deviation Log — photo-mcp

Per `docs/10-process-flow.md` §5, every deviation from the documented
process or requirements MUST be logged here with sponsor approval before
(or immediately upon discovery of) the deviation.

This log is reviewed at every gate and at the final compliance attestation.

---

## Format

```
DEV-<id>
  Date:        <YYYY-MM-DD>
  Rule:        <which §/clause was deviated from>
  What:        <one-line summary of the deviation>
  Why:         <reason>
  Scope:       <one-time | persistent until v1.X | permanent>
  Approved by: <sponsor name + reference to message/signature>
  Approval date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
  Impact:      <effect on requirements, weights, schedule>
  Resolution:  <how it gets reconciled, or "accepted as permanent">
```
